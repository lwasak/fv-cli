# Workflow: issue, show, send, status

`fv` never sends anything by accident. The normal path has four steps, and only the third touches KSeF:

```text
fv issue   ->   fv show   ->   fv send   ->   fv status
 (draft)      (check it)     (to KSeF)     (only if needed)
```

All outputs below are generated by running these commands in-process against fakes of the White List, NBP and KSeF TEST with a fixed clock (2026-10-31 10:00 in Warsaw), and the test `TestTranscript_Guide` fails when this page differs from the real output. The names and numbers (the buyer `acme`, the seller NIP, the KSeF number) are test data.

## Before you start

You need the config from [Setup](setup.md) (seller NIP, `defaults.vat_rate`, `defaults.bank_account`), a buyer from [Parties](parties.md), and, for sending, an imported certificate. The examples assume the config below and the buyer `acme`:

```toml
[ksef]
environment = "test"

[seller]
nip = "5260250274"
place_of_issue = "Gdynia"

[defaults]
vat_rate = "23"
bank_account = "PL61 1090 1014 0000 0712 1981 2874"

[parties.acme]
nip = "5252248481"
```

## 1. Issue a draft

```powershell
fv issue --to acme --net "8500,43"
```

`--net` is the **net** amount in zloty. Quote it: see [Amounts](amounts.md). Everything else comes from the config.

What `fv issue` did, in order:

1. Checked the config and every input. Mistakes stop here, before any lookup or number is used.
2. Looked up the **seller** and the **buyer** in the registries (cached; see [Parties](parties.md)). If a lookup fails, exit code 2 and **no number is taken**.
3. For a foreign currency, fetched the NBP exchange rate ([below](#foreign-currency)).
4. **Reserved the next invoice number** and wrote `invoice.xml` and `meta.json` into the archive ([Numbering and archive](numbering-and-archive.md)). If anything fails after the number was reserved, the number is released again.
5. Printed the invoice, with the environment first, and the hint that nothing was sent.

The invoice is now a **draft**. It owns its number. Look at the preview with care:

- **Environment** on the first line. It is the environment the draft belongs to for good.
- **Sale date.** It follows `defaults.sale_date` (the issue date by default). `last_day_of_month` and `last_day_of_previous_month` fit invoicing at month's end or just after it. Use `--sale-date` to set it by hand.
- **Due date.** Issue date plus `payment_days` (14 by default).
- **Seller and buyer** with VAT status, when the registry was checked and the registry's request id.
- **Account.** `on White List: yes` means the bank account is one the seller published. `NO` comes with a warning: check `bank_account`, because the buyer pays to that account, and paying to an account missing from the White List can cause problems for the buyer.
- **Warnings** at the bottom. fv warns when: the gross amount is 15 000 PLN or more (split payment, MPP, may be mandatory for some goods and services, and fv does not support it); the buyer is not an active VAT payer; the issue date is in the future.

### More ways to say what you sold

```powershell
fv issue --to acme --qty 160 --unit-price "115,50" --desc "Consulting {month_name_en} {year}"
fv issue --to acme --net "2000" --rate 8 --issue-date 2026-10-31 --sale-date 2026-10-30
fv issue --to acme --net "2000" --validate
```

- `--qty` and `--unit-price` give one line with a quantity (up to 6 decimals); use them instead of `--net`, never both.
- `--rate` is the VAT rate: `23`, `8`, `5` or `zw` (exempt). `zw` needs `exemption_basis` in the config, the statutory basis that goes on the invoice. The rates `0` and `np` (reverse charge, export) are **not supported in v1**.
- `--desc` sets the line description. The default is `Usługi za {month_name} {year}`, in Polish; `{month_name_en}` is the English month. The tokens use the **sale date**.
- `--issue-date` and `--sale-date` take `YYYY-MM-DD`. A draft may carry a future issue date, but fv will not send it until that day.
- `--validate` also checks the XML against the FA(3) schema with `xmllint`, which must be on your `PATH`.

One invoice has one line in v1. Corrections, advance invoices and PDF output are not supported.

## 3. Send it

```powershell
fv send FV/2026/10/001
```

fv shows the invoice again, **asks**, and only then sends. On TEST and DEMO the question is `[y/N]`; `--yes` skips it there.

Behind that, fv logs in with your certificate, opens a KSeF online session, **encrypts** the XML (AES-256-CBC, the key wrapped for the Ministry's certificate), posts it once, polls for the verdict for up to **120 seconds**, stores the **KSeF number** and the **UPO** (the official confirmation of receipt, `upo.xml`) in the archive, and closes the session. Before the invoice leaves your machine, the state `sending` and the session reference are already on disk, so a crash cannot lose track of it.

fv sends an invoice to the environment it was issued for. `--env` with another environment is refused, so a TEST draft can never go to PROD.

It refuses to send when:

- the invoice is **already in KSeF**. It is never sent twice;
- its state is `sending` or `unknown`: the answer was lost or is pending, and `fv status` has to resolve it first;
- it was **rejected**: discard it and issue a new invoice;
- the issue date is **after today** (Warsaw): KSeF rejects such invoices.

To issue and send in one go (the preview and the question are the same):

```powershell
fv issue --to acme --net "8500,43" --send
fv issue --to acme --net "8500,43" --send --yes
```

### What can come out of a send

| Result | Exit code | State | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accepted: KSeF number and UPO printed | 0 | `accepted` | Nothing. |
| Refused at the question | 10 | `draft` | Nothing was sent. |
| Rejected by KSeF: code, description, details printed | 4 | `rejected` | Fix the cause, `fv discard`, issue again. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#ksef-rejects-the-invoice) |
| No final answer in 120 s, or the answer was lost | 5 | `unknown` | Run `fv status`. [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#a-send-ends-with-state-unknown) |
| Login or certificate failed | 3 | `draft` | [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#fv-ksef-check-or-send-fails-with-exit-code-3) |

## 4. Status

```powershell
fv status FV/2026/10/001
```

For an accepted invoice that is all local:

<!-- transcript:status -->
```text
$ fv status FV/2026/10/001
FV/2026/10/001 (TEST): accepted by KSeF.
KSeF number: 5260250274-20261031-0100001AF62C-27
Acquired:    2026-10-31T09:00:00Z
UPO:         stored (upo.xml)
```

`fv status` is most useful after exit code 5. For an invoice in state `sending` or `unknown` it logs in and asks KSeF, using the session and invoice references stored before the send:

- **accepted**: stores the KSeF number and fetches the UPO;
- **rejected**: marks it rejected and prints the details (exit code 4);
- **not yet decided**: says so and exits 5. Run it again in a moment;
- **never received**: if the answer to the send was lost, fv finds the invoice in the session by its hash. If KSeF provably never got it (the session is closed or expired and holds no invoice), the invoice goes back to `draft` and you can send it again.

An accepted invoice that has no UPO stored (the fetch failed at send time) gets it fetched by `fv status`. A draft is reported as not sent.

## Environments and production safeguards

| Environment | Use it for | Real consequences |
|---|---|---|
| `test` (default) | Trying things out. Data is shared with other developers. | None. Use made-up data. |
| `demo` | Final check, configured like production. | None. |
| `prod` | Real invoices. | **Legally binding.** KSeF numbers are permanent. |

Choose the environment with `ksef.environment` in the config (default `test`) or per run with the global flag, `fv --env demo send FV/2026/10/001`. The environment is also part of everything fv keeps: numbering counters, the archive, the credential and the keyring entry are all **per environment**. A draft keeps the environment it was issued in.

Safeguards:

- **The environment is the first line of every preview**, in capitals, and in red on a terminal for PROD.
- **Production needs the word.** The question for PROD is:

```text
Send invoice FV/2026/10/001 to the PRODUCTION KSeF? This is real and legally binding. Type PRODUCTION to continue:
```

  Anything but the exact word `PRODUCTION` aborts with exit code 10 and nothing is sent. The question is shown in red on a terminal.
- **`--yes` never skips it.** With `--yes`, fv prints a notice that it does not skip the PRODUCTION confirmation.
- **No terminal, no production send.** Without an interactive terminal (a script, a scheduled task) a send to PROD aborts with exit code 10. So does a send to TEST or DEMO without `--yes`.
- **A draft cannot change environment.** `--env` that differs from the draft's is refused.
- **Registries are always production** and read-only; this does not send anything anywhere.

## Foreign currency

```powershell
fv issue --to acme --net "2000" --currency EUR
```

`--currency` (or `currency` in a party or `[defaults]`) is an ISO code. Amounts are in that currency; the VAT for tax purposes is also shown in zloty.

<!-- transcript:issue-eur -->
```text
$ fv issue --to acme --net 2000 --currency EUR

Seller:    MOJA FIRMA JANUSZ NOWAK
           ul. Świętojańska 10/3, 81-368 Gdynia
           NIP 5260250274
           VAT: Czynny (checked 2026-10-31 10:00, White List requestId s3ll3-r000001)

Buyer:     ACME SPÓŁKA Z OGRANICZONĄ ODPOWIEDZIALNOŚCIĄ
           ul. Prosta 51, 00-838 Warszawa
           NIP 5252248481
           VAT: Czynny (checked 2026-10-31 10:00, White List requestId a1b2c-d3e4f5g)

  No                 Description  Qty  Unit    Price      Net  Rate
   1  Usługi za październik 2026    1  usł.  2000,00  2000,00   23%

  Rate      Net     VAT    Gross  VAT PLN
   23%  2000,00  460,00  2460,00  1955,55
Total:     2460,00 EUR

Account:   PL61 1090 1014 0000 0712 1981 2874  (on White List: yes)
Payment:   transfer (FormaPlatnosci 6)

Nothing was sent to KSeF. To send: fv send FV/2026/10/002
```

How fv gets the rate:

- It uses the **NBP table A mid rate** for the **last business day before the tax point**. The tax point is the sale date, or the issue date when the invoice is issued before the sale date (art. 31a ust. 2 of the VAT act).
- fv asks NBP for the day before the tax point and walks back day by day when there is no table (weekends, holidays), at most 10 days. fv never walks back past a table that is merely not published yet. If the day before the tax point is in the future, or it is today and NBP has no table for today yet (NBP publishes around noon on business days), fv stops with "rate not yet published": issue later, or set `--sale-date`/`--issue-date`. (Walk-back applies only to days that are before today, such as weekends and holidays.)
- The rate and the table number are written to the invoice: each line carries the exchange rate (`KursWaluty`), `P_14_xW` is the VAT in PLN rounded half up, and the additional description says `Kurs waluty: 1 EUR = 4.2512 PLN, tabela 211/A/NBP/2026 z 2026-10-30`.
- A failed NBP call is exit code 2, like a registry failure. An unknown currency code, or no rate within 10 days, is exit code 1.
- Foreign currency invoices support the Polish rates `23`, `8`, `5` and `zw` only. `np` and `0` (reverse charge, intra-community and export supplies) are rejected with a message: **reverse charge is out of v1**.
